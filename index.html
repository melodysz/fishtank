Hi, I'm Melody!

I'm a junior at the University of Pennsylvania studying Design and Consumer Psychology to decode why users click, scroll, and stay.

I specialize in 0-to-1 Product Design and Visual Systems. My goal is to find that sweet spot where functionality meets fun, creating colorful, accessible identities.

My experience spans various industries, including health tech, AI lifestyle, and entertainment. I'm at my best when I'm bridging the gap between design and development, managing creative teams, or facilitating workshops that turn messy ideas into shipped products. I thrive in chatty environments where designers and developers work side-by-side to solve complex problems.

Offline, I read horror novels for fun—because whether it's a scary story or a user journey, I'm obsessed with understanding human reaction.
